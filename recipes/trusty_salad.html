Martin's Trusty Salad

Description

A tomato and salad spiced with salt and pepper and a bit of lemon.

Ingredients

  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • lemon
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. Wash the tomato and onion
  2. Chop to taste
  3. Add salt and pepper to taste
  4. Squeeze the lemon to add the juice
  5. Enjoy!
